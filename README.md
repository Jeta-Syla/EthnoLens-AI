# 🌿 EthnoLens AI
### Turning Centuries of Traditional Medicinal Knowledge into Structured, Searchable Insights with AI.

## 📖 About the Project

**EthnoLens AI** is an AI-powered platform designed to preserve, explore, and structure traditional medicinal knowledge from historical documents, ethnobotanical studies, and scientific literature.

For centuries, communities have passed down knowledge about medicinal plants and their traditional uses. However, much of this valuable information remains scattered across books, manuscripts, research papers, and historical records, making it difficult to access, search, and analyze.

EthnoLens AI aims to bridge this gap by using Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to extract relevant information, identify medicinal plants, and transform unstructured textual knowledge into structured, searchable data.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional medicinal knowledge represents an important part of cultural and scientific heritage. However, accessing and analyzing this information presents several challenges:

- Knowledge is scattered across different historical and scientific sources.
- Many documents are available in unstructured or scanned formats.
- Plant names and traditional uses may vary across regions and languages.
- Manually extracting and organizing information is time-consuming.
- Connections between medicinal plants, traditional uses, and geographic regions are difficult to explore.

## 💡 Our Solution

EthnoLens AI provides a centralized platform that uses AI-powered information extraction to make traditional medicinal knowledge easier to discover and explore.

The platform aims to:

- Extract medicinal plant information from textual documents.
- Identify plant names and their traditional medicinal uses.
- Organize extracted information into structured records.
- Connect plants with geographic regions and historical sources.
- Provide searchable access to documented traditional knowledge.
- Visualize relationships between plants, uses, and regions.

## ⚙️ Key Features

- **AI-Powered Text Extraction:** Identify relevant information from historical and ethnobotanical documents.
- **Medicinal Plant Recognition:** Extract and organize plant names and scientific classifications.
- **Traditional Use Identification:** Identify documented traditional applications of medicinal plants.
- **Smart Search:** Search for plants, traditional uses, and geographic information.
- **Knowledge Organization:** Transform unstructured information into structured records.
- **Source Traceability:** Connect extracted information to its original documentary source.
- **Interactive Knowledge Exploration:** Explore relationships between plants, uses, and regions.

## 🧠 How It Works

The proposed workflow consists of five main stages:

1. **Document Collection** – Gather historical documents, ethnobotanical studies, and scientific publications.
2. **Text Processing** – Extract readable text using document processing and OCR when necessary.
3. **AI & NLP Extraction** – Identify medicinal plants, traditional uses, locations, and relevant information.
4. **Data Structuring** – Organize extracted information into structured and searchable records.
5. **Knowledge Exploration** – Allow users to search, filter, and explore the collected knowledge.
